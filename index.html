Summary

QA Engineer at a product company, working on the quality of consumer mobile and web products. I see quality not as a final checkpoint but as a way of thinking that runs through the whole product process: clear requirements, predictable releases, and decisions backed by data.

I know the routine of day-to-day QA from the inside: analysing tasks, preparing checklists, writing bug reports, assembling release summaries and reporting on results. That hands-on experience showed me which parts of the work are repetitive and which truly need a human, so I started bringing AI into this routine, and I keep refining that direction by creating new skills that cover more and more of my daily tasks. I work with AI assistants every day and keep teaching them to work with the tools the team lives in, like ClickUp, Notion and Slack, while I keep building a technical knowledge base of the product that people and AI can both rely on.

My fascination with programming has evolved into working with AI, where I, as an engineer, set the goals and the rules and stay in control of the final result. It satisfies my need to create and lifts my mood at the same time. And as AI keeps finding its way into more and more tools, what began as a hobby is turning into an essential professional skill. So I head to work almost with joy!

P.S. I haven’t given up on the dream of testing a product that changes the world for the better. When it comes along, it will find me with the checklist ready.
